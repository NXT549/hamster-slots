# Changelog

Everything that changes in **Hamster Slots**, written for players.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the game uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html): a patch (0.1.1) fixes things, a minor version (0.2.0) adds features or content, and 1.0.0 is the full public release.

**Every update has a name** that says what it's about, after its version and date (and in the game's Menu).

All coins in the game are pretend. There's no real money in it, and nothing to buy with real money.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- **Later colonies last longer.** After a Great Migration, each new colony's Family Tree costs three times as much as the last colony's, and **Colony Pride** gives **+30% payouts a level** (was +50%). A migrated family used to rebuild everything in a few lives and race through hamsters lasting two or three minutes; now the second colony's hamsters live several minutes each. Your first colony doesn't change, and traits you've already planted stay planted.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-09-28 · The Great Migration

**A mega rebirth for the late game: once your Family Tree is fully grown, the whole family can move to a new colony and start again, with Golden Whiskers to spend on perks that last forever. Plus Colony Trials, the Wise Elders (who retire for you), an 8th machine, and longer late lives.**

### Added

- **The Great Migration.** Plant the whole Family Tree and your family can move to a new colony (from the Big Cage, or Family → Colony). The generation, Heirloom Seeds, the tree and Machine Stars start again, and the family takes **Golden Whiskers** with it: the more seeds it earned in the colony, the more whiskers. Your skins, tokens, diary stickers, casino chips and stats come along.

Reach the trial's seed goal for Golden Whiskers, and the twist is over for the rest of that life. Each trial pays once per colony.
- **The Wise Elders.** A perk that retires your hamster for you when its seeds are ready (you choose when), plants the cheap traits and starts the next life, all without stopping.

- **6 new diary stickers:** New Horizons, Far, Far Away, Trial by Fur, School of Hard Knocks, Wise Old Hamster and Box Full.
- Golden Whiskers show next to your coins and seeds once you have some. New art: the Moving Box, the Moving Day machine, golden whiskers and the elders' spectacles, and new sounds for boxes opening and for the migration.

### Changed

- **Late lives last longer.** Past about 100 Heirloom Seeds, each new seed takes more coins than before, so the lives from about the 11th generation on stop shrinking to a couple of minutes. Seeds now come from the coins earned in the current colony. **Your saved game keeps its progress:** the seeds you could retire with right now stay exactly the same.
- The Family tab now says how many more coins the next Heirloom Seed needs.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-09-27 · The Hamster Casino

**The Hamster Casino opens: roulette, blackjack, a hamster derby and Seed Drop.**

### Added

- **The Hamster Casino** (a new Casino tab). It opens when your family retires for the first time, with **250 chips** to start.
- **Casino Chips:** your hamster earns a chip every 2 paid spins, and the family gets 250 more whenever a hamster retires. You can also buy chips with coins at the cashier. Chips are pretend like the coins, and they only buy prizes: they never turn back into coins.

- **Seed Drop:** drop seeds down the pegs into the bins. The edge bins pay ×12, if you're lucky.
- **Every table says what it pays and what it gives back on average.** Like a real casino, the house keeps a small edge (you get back 95–99% of your chips on average).
- **The Prize Counter:** Golden Hour (every machine pays +50% for a while), Turbo Wheel (faster spins for a while), the Lucky Charm (+15 Luck for your next 100 paid spins), Token Bags, and three skins you can only get here: the **Dealer's Visor**, the **Tuxedo** fur and the **Casino Night** room. ## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-25 · First Spin

The first numbered version: everything built so far.

### Added

- **A hamster-powered slot machine.** Your hamster runs on its wheel to power the machine. Spins cost coins; winning lines pay coins.
- **Four slot machines**, each with its own look: **Old Clunky** (2 reels, and you can bolt on a third), the **Snack Stacker** (3 rows and up to 5 paylines), the **Burrow Bonanza** (5 reels, Hamster Wilds and free spins) and the **Pouch Palace** (up to 20 paylines and a jackpot wheel). Buy new machines and switch between them any time.
- **A real pokie feel.** Spins take about 3 seconds, and the reels stop one at a time with a clunk. After a win, every winning line lights up in turn with what it paid, while a WIN meter counts up. Tap the reels to skip.
- **Food deliveries.** Out of coins? Send your hamster on a delivery. It always comes back with coins, so you can never get stuck.
- **Upgrades** for your hamster and for each machine: bigger payouts, cheaper spins, auto-spin (Wheel Training), more reels and paylines. Buy one level at a time, ×10 or Max, and see how long until you can afford the next one.
- **Symbols to unlock.** Every machine starts with its two best symbols locked. Unlocking them means bigger prizes but rarer wins.
- **Luck** you can see: Hamster Luck (the Four-Leaf Clover) plus Machine Luck (a lucky charm for each machine). More Luck means more wins, and the machine shows its Luck number.
- **Bets from ×1 to ×10**, unlocked with the High Roller upgrade. A bigger bet costs more and pays more.
- **Bonus features:** the **Hamster Wild** (stands in for other symbols), **free spins** (land 3 Hamster Balls; every win is ×2), the **jackpot wheel** with four growing pots (Mini, Minor, Major, Grand), **Hot Streak** (wins in a row pay more), and the **card gamble**: after a win you pulled yourself, guess red or black to double it, or the suit for ×4.
- **Retirement and the Family Tree.** When your hamster has earned enough, it can retire to the Big Cage. A new pup takes over, and you get Heirloom Seeds to plant in the Family Tree for 11 permanent family traits. Every new pup starts stronger than the last.
- **Hamster Tokens and the Capsule Machine.** Earn tokens from 31 Hamster Diary stickers (goals), golden jackpots, deliveries and retiring. Spend them on capsules for 22 skins (fur, wheels, machines and rooms), and dress up your hamster in the Wardrobe. The odds are always shown, and a guaranteed Epic comes within 20 pulls.
- **Offline earnings.** While you're away, your hamster keeps working at half speed for up to 2 hours (once it has learned Wheel Training), and a welcome-back message shows what it earned.
