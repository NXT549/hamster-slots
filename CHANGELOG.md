# Changelog

Everything that changes in **Hamster Slots**, written for players.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the game uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html): a patch (0.1.1) fixes things, a minor version (0.2.0) adds features or content, and 1.0.0 will be the full public release.

All coins in the game are pretend. There's no real money in it, and nothing to buy with real money.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Save backup** (Menu → Save backup): your whole save as a one-line code to copy and keep somewhere safe. Paste a code back in to load it: to get your hamster back, or to carry on in another browser or on another computer. It tells you what's in a code before you load it.

### Changed

- **No more upper limit.** Coins, Heirloom Seeds and Hamster Tokens can now grow far past 1.8e308, where they used to turn into "Infinity". Your save carries over as it is.
- Very big numbers, from a quadrillion up, are now written like **1.23e15** (that's 1.23 × 10¹⁵), instead of piling up as 1,000T and beyond. That goes for coins, Heirloom Seeds and Hamster Tokens.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-25

The first numbered version: everything built so far.

### Added

- **A hamster-powered slot machine.** Your hamster runs on its wheel to power the machine. Spins cost coins; winning lines pay coins.
- **Four slot machines**, each with its own look: **Old Clunky** (2 reels, and you can bolt on a third), the **Snack Stacker** (3 rows and up to 5 paylines), the **Burrow Bonanza** (5 reels, Hamster Wilds and free spins) and the **Pouch Palace** (up to 20 paylines and a jackpot wheel). Buy new machines and switch between them any time.
- **A real pokie feel.** Spins take about 3 seconds, and the reels stop one at a time with a clunk. After a win, every winning line lights up in turn with what it paid, while a WIN meter counts up. Tap the reels to skip.
- **Food deliveries.** Out of coins? Send your hamster on a delivery. It always comes back with coins, so you can never get stuck.
- **Upgrades** for your hamster and for each machine: bigger payouts, cheaper spins, auto-spin (Wheel Training), more reels and paylines. Buy one level at a time, ×10 or Max, and see how long until you can afford the next one.
- **Symbols to unlock.** Every machine starts with its two best symbols locked. Unlocking them means bigger prizes but rarer wins.
- **Luck** you can see: Hamster Luck (the Four-Leaf Clover) plus Machine Luck (a lucky charm for each machine). More Luck means more wins, and the machine shows its Luck number.
- **Bets from ×1 to ×10**, unlocked with the High Roller upgrade. A bigger bet costs more and pays more.
- **Bonus features:** the **Hamster Wild** (stands in for other symbols), **free spins** (land 3 Hamster Balls; every win is ×2), the **jackpot wheel** with four growing pots (Mini, Minor, Major, Grand), **Hot Streak** (wins in a row pay more), and the **card gamble**: after a win you pulled yourself, guess red or black to double it, or the suit for ×4.
- **Retirement and the Family Tree.** When your hamster has earned enough, it can retire to the Big Cage. A new pup takes over, and you get Heirloom Seeds to plant in the Family Tree for 11 permanent family traits. Every new pup starts stronger than the last.
- **Hamster Tokens and the Capsule Machine.** Earn tokens from 31 Hamster Diary stickers (goals), golden jackpots, deliveries and retiring. Spend them on capsules for 22 skins (fur, wheels, machines and rooms), and dress up your hamster in the Wardrobe. The odds are always shown, and a guaranteed Epic comes within 20 pulls.
- **Offline earnings.** While you're away, your hamster keeps working at half speed for up to 2 hours (once it has learned Wheel Training), and a welcome-back message shows what it earned.
- **Autosave** every 10 seconds and whenever you leave the page.
- **Cute extras:** the hamster-cage look, speech-bubble hints from your hamster, sound effects with a volume control, pixel sparkles and confetti, win banners, a Stats screen, and an Info tab with the paytable, the paylines, the real odds of every feature, and your recent wins.
- **Settings:** less motion, quick reels, short or full numbers.
